## Professional outreach, built for PROCESS IN

**MailBoost** is an internal email outreach and campaign operations platform developed for **[PROCESS IN](https://processin.mk)**. Its purpose is to bring business offers, message preparation and campaign coordination into one organized workspace.

The idea is straightforward: **a strong business offer deserves a professional delivery process**. Instead of treating email outreach as a collection of disconnected tasks, MailBoost gives PROCESS IN a dedicated operational product identity and a clearer workflow.

**MailBoost is an internal tool, not a publicly sold SaaS.** Its name and positioning do not imply guaranteed inbox placement, delivery rates or customer acquisition results.

## From an offer to an organized campaign

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **01 — Define the audience** | Establish who the communication is relevant for and why |
| **02 — Prepare the offer** | Present a clear value proposition, business identity and next step |
| **03 — Compose the message** | Use professional, readable and brand-consistent email presentation |
| **04 — Organize the campaign** | Structure outreach work and manage the intended sending process |
| **05 — Review operations** | Examine available activity information and identify follow-up needs |
| **06 — Improve the next campaign** | Refine targeting, copy and workflow using responsible operational feedback |

*This describes the intended operating workflow, not a claim that every step is already automated or that all reporting capabilities are live.*

## Designed for an internal business workflow

MailBoost supports the **PROCESS IN business development and outreach workflow**. It is not marketed as a third-party sending service, an email database, a lead-harvesting system or a promise to bypass spam filtering.

Its product identity follows the wider PROCESS IN design language: **black, blue, white, Inter-inspired typography, disciplined spacing and functional simplicity**.
