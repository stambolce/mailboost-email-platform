## Focused outreach. Clear communication.

**MailBoost** is an **internal PROCESS IN platform** for organizing business email outreach and delivering relevant offers to recipients. It is designed around a clear operational goal: help PROCESS IN manage professional outbound communication through one dedicated system.

**MailBoost is not presented as a publicly available SaaS, third-party bulk-email service, or an inbox placement guarantee.** The product name describes its intended communication purpose; successful inbox delivery depends on recipient consent and relevance, sender reputation, authentication and receiving providers.

## Product focus

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Business outreach** | Organize direct business communications and offers |
| **Campaign workflows** | Structure outbound email activity |
| **Message presentation** | Deliver consistent, professional branded email content |
| **Contact organization** | Support relevant, permission-aware recipient workflows |
| **Operational visibility** | Provide a clear overview of campaign activity |
| **Delivery practices** | Prioritize responsible sending, authentication and reputation |

> This is a high-level public description of internal product goals, not a verified inventory of implemented functionality. Specific technical controls, integrations and campaign metrics are intentionally not disclosed.

## Operating principles

- **Relevant communication:** Send business messages to appropriate recipients.
- **Responsible sending:** Respect applicable email rules, opt-out requests and provider policies.
- **Brand consistency:** Maintain a professional PROCESS IN visual identity.
- **Operational clarity:** Keep outreach work organized and manageable.
- **Privacy by design:** Do not expose recipient records or sensitive infrastructure publicly.
